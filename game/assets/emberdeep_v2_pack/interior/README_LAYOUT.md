B is a walkable bridge with lava on both sides. S is where the player spawns. E is the only exit. H is the hoard. N is the nest. K is the boss door, and it counts as a floor tile.

The entry hall is the wide room along the south. The player stands on S. The exit E is the very next tile south, set into the south wall. North, east, and west of the spawn are open floor, so the spawn is not boxed in. A three-tile corridor leaves the north side of the hall.

That corridor opens into the lava crossing. Most of the room is lava. A three-tile bridge runs north to south through it, with lava on both sides. A three-tile stone path crosses the bridge from east to west so the side rooms can be reached without stepping in the lava.

The treasury is the room to the west of the crossing. H sits near the middle of that room. A small lava patch occupies the northwest corner and does not block the hoard or the three-tile doorway.

The nest is the alcove to the east. N sits in that alcove. A little lava lies along the back wall, clear of the nest and clear of the three-tile doorway.

North of the lava room, a three-tile link enters the long gallery. The gallery is a north-south spine three tiles wide. Stone niches open off both sides, and wall buttresses stand between the niches so the gallery does not collapse into one wide room.

The boss chamber is the room at the far north. It is wider than the corridor and cut back at the corners. The threshold is three tiles wide. K is the center tile of that doorway.

Every special tile can be walked to from the spawn. Corridors stay three tiles wide. The same grid is the layout for all eight floors until someone authors more.
