# Crownfall patch notes

## 17 October 2026: combat levels, moving fishing spots, idle logout

### Monster levels now use the same scale as yours
- Monster levels are now worked out the same way as your combat level: Attack + Strength + Defence. Tougher hides, extra hitpoints and slow swings nudge the number up or down. Bosses and dragonfire add 10%. Gear never counts.
- Monsters have not got stronger. Their old numbers just undersold them by about 3×. Knight-Captain Vorn used to say 92 and now says **337**. The Adamant Dragon says **377** and Nyxarath **1100**.
- Cavern and Ember beasts show their real level too. A "lvl 50" cavern beast is now labelled **lvl 125**.
- Loot is unchanged.
- Nameplate colours now compare the monster's level with yours:
  - **green**: 90% of your level or less, so you should win;
  - **yellow**: up to 110%, an even fight;
  - **orange**: up to 130%, risky;
  - **red**: above that.

### Fishing spots move
- Every fishing spot has a shared stock of 10–20 catches. Everyone fishing it draws from the same pool.
- A spot also runs out 20–40 seconds after its first catch, whichever comes first.
- When it runs out, "The fishing spot moves away.", you stop fishing, and it reappears 9–15 seconds later on a shoreline 3–6 tiles from its usual place. Look for the splash.

### Idle logout
- If you do nothing for 5 minutes, you are logged out. At 4:30 a banner and chime warn you, and any action cancels it.
- You won't be logged out mid-fight: the logout waits until 10 seconds after combat ends.
- If you disconnect mid-fight, your character stays in the world for 10 seconds after the last hit, or 60 seconds if you're PK-flagged. It can still be attacked during that time. You can't log back in until it has gone.

### Gathering animations
- Woodcutting, mining and fishing now play their chop, mine and fish animations. Before, your character just stood idle.
- The client is ready for the new 12-frame, 8-direction strips (including net, harpoon and lobster pot) as soon as the art lands.
