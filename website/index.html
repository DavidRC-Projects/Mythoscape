How to play

No Terminal. Opening Crownfall starts the game server for you.

  1. Download Unlock with your invite code, then download the slim Mac build. Wait until the zip finishes before opening it.
  2. Open Crownfall Unzip, then right-click Crownfall → Open (required once on macOS for unsigned apps). Or double-click Play Crownfall in the same folder.
  3. Play The app starts a local server, then the login screen. Create an account and walk into the world. Quitting Crownfall stops the server.
